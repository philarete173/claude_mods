Session panel v2: visual preview

The redesigned session pane, drawn from the element trees in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-session-panel-visual.md by a small stand-in for the desktop renderer. One cell is 8 px, as measured on the live desktop, and text uses your system font. Click a ▾ to fold a section. Drag the width to check that no row crosses the right edge.

Desktop, dark theme

The sample of current-pane-v1.png: $148.59 in total, Context at 88.2 %, six subagents with one running, five MCP servers, twelve log lines. The title bar belongs to the app.

Desktop, light theme

The same markup with the light theme's colours. Icons, rules and meters are SVG images, so they keep their colours in both themes.

Other states

A new session before the first response; and a session with plan limits, a nearly full context, three folded sections and every subagent status.

Terminal surface

The terminal has no Svg: rules become ─ lines, meters become ▰▱ cells, icons drop out, status marks are glyphs and the project line keeps its emoji. The tree is otherwise the same.
